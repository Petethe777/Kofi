Part 01

How Kofi Began

Sofia, trained as a pastry chef in Paris with a focus on botanicals and aromatics, and Mateo, a third-generation coffee roaster from Colombia, first crossed paths in Miami’s creative scene. On an eye-opening backpacking trip through Kyoto, they experienced authentic chasen-whisked ceremonial matcha inside a 300-year-old preserved wooden teahouse tucked in Wazuka.

They realized Miami was overrun with overly sweet, artificially sweetened powders masquerading as matcha. The market was completely missing the delicate umami, vivid emerald hue, and serene, jitter-free calm of genuine single-origin green tea. They returned determined to change the city’s morning ritual.

Part 02

Building the Mobile Cart

In late 2022, they sourced and meticulously restored a handcrafted cedar wood cart. Rather than opting for noisy commercial generators, they engineered a silent, solar-charged high-capacity lithium battery bank to run commercial Italian dual-boiler espresso equipment and precision temperature-controlled hot water taps.

Featuring a dedicated bamboo chasen whisking bar and custom ceramic bowls, the mobile cart lets them bring authentic, zero-emission Japanese and Colombian cafe craft straight to neighborhood sidewalks, artisan design districts, and sandy beachside sunrises.
