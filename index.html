The Human Touch Behind Every Cup

Hand-whisked with Love: Meet Sofia & Mateo

What started as a tiny vintage bicycle cart in Coconut Grove has blossomed into Miami's beloved mobile matcha destination. After traveling across tea estates in Kyoto and coffee fincas in Antioquia, we set out to craft wholesome, vibrant drinks that honor sacred ritual with sunny Miami vibrancy.

Every syrup is simmered in-house, and every single cup is hand-whisked to order. We believe morning coffee shouldn't just be fuel—it should be a bright, serene pause in your day.
