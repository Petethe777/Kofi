Frequent Questions

Cart Etiquette & Logistics

Quick details on how we roll, ordering ahead, weather contingency plans, and our payments setup.

Do you accept card, cash, or Apple Pay at the cart?

expand_more

We operate completely frictionless contactless payments. We happily accept Apple Pay, Google Pay, all major credit cards, and tap debit cards. We do not carry cash change boxes on board our mobile cart for safety and speedy coastal service.

Can I order ahead for pickup during busy hours?

expand_more

Yes! Pre-orders open 30 minutes before our scheduled cart arrival each morning. Simply tap the "Order Ahead" button next to our active stop to select your oat milk, sweetness grade, or cold brew concentrate, and skip the sidewalk queue.

What happens if it rains in Miami?

expand_more

Miami afternoon storms are part of the charm! Our restored trailer is outfitted with a custom waterproof canvas awning and covered queue umbrella. In cases of severe tropical lightning or gale wind warnings, we shift to indoor partner locations and blast updates live to our Instagram stories (@koficartmiami) and SMS radar.

What kind of milk options and dietary choices do you bring?

expand_more

Every day we stock creamy barista-edition oat milk, house-pressed organic almond milk, pistachio milk, and grass-fed local dairy. All our matcha sweeteners are made with pure organic agave or wild orange blossom honey. 100% refined sugar-free.
